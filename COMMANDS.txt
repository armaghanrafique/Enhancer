================================================================================
 6. QUALITY KNOBS  (turn these UP for better results, DOWN for speed)
================================================================================
  --sr-model          realesrgan-x4plus (max detail) | diffusion-x4 (best, slow)
                      | realesr-general-x4v3 (fast) | spandrel (HAT/DRCT/SwinIR)
  --outscale          4 = best (whole frame). 2 = faster.
  --scan-outscale     plate scan zoom: 4 default; 6-8 for tiny/far plates
  --plate-sr-model    realesrgan-x4plus for the sharpest plate crops
  --plate-mode        scan = best for far plates | crop = for close-up plates
  --min-plate-len     5 filters out short noise / clock digits
  --steps             diffusion only: 60+ for best quality (very slow)
  --tile              0 = no seams (best) if it fits memory; else 256 / 128
  --smooth            0.2 video flicker reduction
  --no-deep           OFF (never use for best quality)


================================================================================
 7. WHAT CANNOT BE FIXED (physics, not settings)
================================================================================
  A plate must be READABLE in the source pixels. No model can recover:
    - blown-out / overexposed plates (clipped to white)
    - near-black plates with no detail
    - heavy motion blur that erased the characters
  If you can't read it by eye after zooming, OCR won't either.
  For those, capture a better frame (different frame of the video often helps).
